About the Neonatal GIR & Nutrition Calculator

Purpose

The Neonatal GIR & Nutrition Calculator is a browser-based calculation aid for neonatal glucose infusion rate, total fluid intake, nutrition contribution and fluid concentration mixing.

The project is designed to make commonly repeated calculations easier to perform and review while keeping the calculation process visible to the clinician.

Author

Created by Dr. Damam Sreeharsha, MBBS, MD Pediatrics.

The calculator is developed as an educational and clinical calculation aid. Product compositions and institutional practices can vary, so users should verify the exact product and local protocol before clinical use.

Safety and scope

This website is not a prescribing system and is not presented as a validated medical device. It does not replace clinical assessment, institutional protocols, pharmacy verification, product labels or professional judgment. Calculated values should be independently checked before patient care.

Privacy

The calculator is designed to perform calculations locally in the browser. It does not intentionally collect or store patient information.
