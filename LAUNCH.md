## Google Search Console
Manual account verification is required because it must be performed from the owner's Google account.

1. Open Google Search Console.
2. Add a URL-prefix property for the public calculator URL.
3. Complete the verification method Google offers.
4. Open Sitemaps and submit:
   `sitemap.xml`
5. Use URL Inspection for the home page and request indexing.
6. Recheck indexing status after Google has crawled the site.

## Suggested launch post
I built a free neonatal GIR & Nutrition Calculator for bedside calculation support.

It brings together:
- glucose infusion rate (GIR)
- total fluid intake (TFI)
- current IV/oral fluid contributions
- energy, protein/AA and carbohydrate calculations
- TFI-aware target GIR planning
- fluid mixing calculations

It is a calculation aid, not a prescribing system or validated medical device. Product identity, concentration, compatibility, line access, preparation and local NICU protocols should always be verified before administration.

Calculator:
https://amoxyclav.github.io/neonatal-gir-calculator/

Feedback and corrections are welcome through the GitHub issue tracker.

## Feedback loop
When users report a calculation or product-data issue:
1. Reproduce the case.
2. Check the underlying formula or product source.
3. Correct the implementation.
4. Run automated validation.
5. Deploy the correction.
6. Document clinically important changes.
