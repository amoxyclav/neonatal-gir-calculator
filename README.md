# Neonatal GIR & Nutrition Calculator

A free web-based neonatal calculation aid for GIR, total fluid intake (TFI), nutrition contribution and fluid mixing.

Website:
https://amoxyclav.github.io/neonatal-gir-calculator/

## Features

- Weight, TFI and target GIR inputs
- Current IV/oral fluid tracking
- GIR, energy, protein/amino-acid and carbohydrate calculations
- TFI-aware interactive target GIR planning
- D5-first automatic planning within configured stock limits
- Editable composition for permitted products and user-defined custom fluids
- Nutrition totals with HMF, HMF-Advance and MCT oil
- Energy-Protein ratio calculation
- New-bag and existing-bag fluid mixing calculations
- Mobile-friendly static web application
- No login required

## Safety

This is a calculation aid, not a prescribing system or validated medical device. Verify product identity, concentration, compatibility, line access, preparation and local NICU protocols before clinical use.

Product compositions are configurable where permitted. The calculator should not be used as a substitute for clinical judgment, institutional protocols, product labels or pharmacy guidance.

## Privacy

The calculator is designed to process patient inputs locally in the browser. It does not intentionally collect or store patient information.
