Neonatal Fluid Mixer: New Bag and Existing Bag Calculations

Guide to the concentration-mixing calculations used by the neonatal fluid mixer, including new-bag and existing-bag modes.

Making a new bag

The calculator can determine how much of two stock solutions is needed to prepare a chosen final concentration and final volume when the target lies between the two stock concentrations.

Volume of stock A = final volume × (stock B − target) ÷ (stock B − stock A)
Volume of stock B = final volume − volume of stock A

The two stock concentrations must be different, and the target concentration must lie between them.

Adjusting an existing bag

The second mixer mode calculates the amount of a selected stock solution that can be added to an existing solution to reach a target concentration, when that target is physically achievable by addition.

The final volume changes when stock is added. The final concentration is recalculated from the combined amount of solute divided by the final volume.

Clinical verification

The mixer performs concentration arithmetic only. It does not verify sterility, compatibility, osmolarity, stability, precipitation, container compatibility, line requirements, pharmacy preparation procedures or institutional policy. The final preparation must be independently verified before administration.

Why the calculator asks for the actual product

Different products can have different concentrations or nutritional compositions. Always select the exact stock concentration being used and compare the calculated result with the preparation instructions and product label.
