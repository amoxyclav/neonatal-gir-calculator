Neonatal Fluid and Nutrition Calculator: Energy, Protein and Carbohydrate

How the neonatal calculator summarizes TFI, energy, protein, carbohydrate and Energy-Protein ratio from entered nutrition sources.

What the nutrition calculator summarizes

The Nutrition section combines the selected current fluids with the active GIR plan and optional additional nutrition sources. It reports total energy, protein/amino acids and carbohydrate, both per day and per kilogram per day.

It also calculates an Energy-Protein ratio as total calories divided by total protein/amino-acid grams included in the calculation.

Why source composition matters

Every selected source has a composition used by the calculation engine. Preset values are intended as starting values and should be checked against the actual product label, formulation and local stock. Editable fields are provided for products whose concentration or composition may vary.

Examples in the calculator include breast milk, formula milk, Isolyte P, Aminoven, Intralipid, HMF, HMF-Advance and MCT oil.

Energy, protein and carbohydrate

For fluid sources, daily nutrient contribution is calculated from the entered volume and the composition per 100 mL. For sachet or mL-based additional sources, the entered quantity is multiplied by the stored composition per unit.

The resulting totals are summaries of the values entered; they are not a recommendation for an individual infant's energy, protein, carbohydrate or fluid prescription.

Energy-Protein ratio

Energy-Protein ratio = total included energy (kcal/day) ÷ total included protein/amino acids (g/day)

The ratio includes the current fluids, the active automatic GIR plan and selected additional nutrition sources. It should not be interpreted without considering whether all clinically relevant nutrition sources have been entered.

Product-data verification

Product composition can vary by formulation and manufacturer. Confirm the label of the exact product being used, including concentration, amino-acid content, carbohydrate content, energy density and lipid formulation where relevant.

Reference examples

  • Recent Indian clinical literature reports the composition of 1 g Lactodex-HMF as approximately 3.4 kcal, 0.27 g protein and 0.49 g carbohydrate, and HMF-Advance as 4.5 kcal, 0.35 g protein equivalent and 0.24 g carbohydrate per 1 g sachet.
  • Simyl-MCT Oil product information lists approximately 780 kcal per 100 mL and no protein or carbohydrate.
